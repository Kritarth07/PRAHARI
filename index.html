RESOURCES

Report a scam

If you've received a suspicious call or message, or already lost money to one, here's exactly what to do next.

In an active digital‑arrest call right now?

  1. Disconnect the call. Real agencies never conduct arrests over video call.
  2. Do not share an OTP, PIN, or make any payment.
  3. Call the National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
  4. Report online at cybercrime.gov.in

Already made a payment?

Call 1930 immediately — funds can sometimes be frozen if reported within the first few hours.

Not sure if it's a scam?

Run the message through the Scam Detector or chat with Fraud Shield first.

Reporting channels

  • Helpline: 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline)
  • Online: cybercrime.gov.in
  • Your nearest police station or cyber cell
  • Your bank's fraud reporting line, for payment reversal

Protect others

Forward suspicious messages to friends and family who may be less familiar with these tactics.
